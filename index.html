FAQ

The useful details.

Who can enter?

Adults who can legally participate in a promotional giveaway in their country may enter. Entries are currently accepted worldwide, subject to local laws and shipping availability.

What can I add to my wishlist?

Add up to three product URLs from Olive Young. The combined product value should be no more than $100 before shipping, taxes, or duties.

How is the winner selected?

Entries are reviewed based on authenticity, clarity, and the story behind the wishlist. This is not a points competition, and submitting more links does not improve your chances.

How will the winner be contacted?

The selected entrant will be contacted using the email submitted in the form. Never send payment or banking information in response to a winner notification.

Is DropTheWish affiliated with Olive Young?

No. DropTheWish is an independent project and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Olive Young or any brand displayed on this page.

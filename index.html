FAQ

The small print,
without the headache.

Who can enter?

Anyone in a country where Olive Young Global can reasonably ship may enter, subject to the final giveaway rules.

How is the winner selected?

Entries are reviewed based on authenticity, clarity, and the story behind the wishlist. It is not random unless the published rules say otherwise.

Can I submit more than one product?

Yes. You may submit up to three product links, provided the final selected wishlist stays within the stated prize limit.

Do I need to buy anything?

No purchase is required to submit an entry.

How will I know if I am selected?

We will contact selected entrants using the email address provided in the form.

Is DropTheWish affiliated with Olive Young?

No. DropTheWish is an independent project and is not sponsored by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Olive Young.

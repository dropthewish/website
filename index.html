FAQ

The questions
people actually ask.

Is this really free?

Yes. No purchase or payment is required to submit an entry.

Will you actually buy the selected products?

The selected entrant may receive eligible Olive Young products from the submitted wishlist, up to a combined product value of $100. Availability and shipping restrictions may require substitutions or another practical arrangement.

How is the selected entry chosen?

Entries are reviewed for authenticity, clarity, and the reason behind the wishlist. This is not a points contest, and adding more products does not improve the chance of selection.

When will the first winner be announced?

The first round is currently open. The selected entrant will be contacted directly by email after the review period. A public date should be added here once the campaign schedule is fixed.

Can anyone enter?

Adults who can legally participate in a promotional giveaway in their location may enter, subject to local laws and shipping availability.

Is DropTheWish affiliated with Olive Young?

No. DropTheWish is an independent project and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Olive Young or any referenced brand.

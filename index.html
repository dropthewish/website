FAQ

The questions
people actually ask.

Is this real?

Yes. DropTheWish is an independent giveaway project. We review submitted stories, contact the selected entrant directly by email, and arrange the eligible product purchase and delivery.

Is this really free?

Yes. No purchase, payment, credit card, or paid membership is required to submit an entry.

How is the selected entry chosen?

Entries are reviewed for authenticity, clarity, and the reason behind the wishlist. This is not a points contest, and adding more products does not improve the chance of selection.

What happens if my wishlist costs more than $100?

The selected entrant may receive up to $100 toward eligible Olive Young products from the submitted wishlist. If the selected wishlist exceeds $100, the recipient may pay the remaining balance. Availability and shipping restrictions may require substitutions, exclusions, or another practical arrangement.

When will the first selection be announced?

The first round is currently open. The selected entrant will be contacted directly by email after the review period.

Can anyone enter?

Adults who can legally participate in a promotional giveaway in their location may enter, subject to local laws and shipping availability.

Is DropTheWish affiliated with Olive Young?

No. DropTheWish is an independent project and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Olive Young or any referenced brand.
